      Settings

      Needs “Maps JavaScript API” and “Routes API” turned on in Google Cloud and allowed on the key. Leave empty to use demo mode.

      This is the standard time at a stop (new stops use it). You can change any one stop's time in the stop list by tapping its “Stop time” button. A stop's time is added to the ETAs after it. Adjustment 100% = use Google's drive time as is; 110% = 10% longer.

      While driving, the app asks Google for fresh times this often and counts down on its own in between (less often = lower Google cost). “⟳ Update now” on the driving screen asks right away, at most once a minute.

      When the GPS shows the bus within about a quarter mile of the next stop and not moving, it is marked dropped off after this many minutes, with an Undo button. Driving past a stop never marks it. The “Dropped off” button always works too.
